### **PERSONAL STATEMENT**

I am a social scientist whose interests largely focus on human motivation and performance.  My purpose in life is to contribute to the scientific body of knowledge by studying individuals in their natural performance environments (a la Brunswikian representative design) and measuring them with the best possible tools.  That purpose guides me as a scientist, father, adventurer, and citizen.  I am an unabashed proponent of quantitative methods \- both frequentists and subjectivists \- using the most sophisticated, open-source tools to achieve my goals.  My work to date and research interests are best explored on [my Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=sH44LC4AAAAJ), [personal](http://www.patrickemcknight.com/), and [MRES lab](http://mres-gmu.org) web sites.
